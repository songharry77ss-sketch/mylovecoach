2. Sending Information for AI Analysis and International Transfers

When you request coaching, the information needed for that request (conversation screenshots, situation description, your profile and the other person's profile, recent coaching context and the reply style and cautions you previously requested in the same chat, and that chat's "What My Love Coach Remembers") is sent over encrypted communication (HTTPS) through the App's relay server (operated on servers of Vercel Inc.) first to the Claude API of Anthropic PBC, our AI processing contractor, and is used to generate an answer. If Anthropic cannot answer because of usage limits, congestion, or errors, the same request is sent to the OpenAI API of OpenAI, L.L.C., and if that also fails, to the Gemini API of Google LLC, to generate the answer. This order applies only to apps that have received the AI analysis consent describing this order (the November 26, 2026 version). Apps that received an earlier version of the consent send requests to Google (Gemini) first, as consented, and send them to Anthropic and OpenAI only when Google cannot answer; older apps that consented only to Google send requests only to Google. When a direct connection to these companies' APIs is unavailable (credit exhaustion, outages, etc.), the same request is sent through BizRouter (operated by AI3), a Korean AI relay service, to a model of the company you consented to (default: Google Gemini) to generate the answer. BizRouter is a processing contractor that forwards requests to the relevant AI company. The relay server does not keep the body of AI requests after forwarding them. Separate operational diagnostics and, if you consented to "Save Coaching Content," coaching records are stored under Section 4. In the same way, the following features also send only the information needed for AI processing: Crush Report (the other person's and your profile, coaching summaries and interest-level history stored on your device, "What My Love Coach Remembers"), Practice Chat (the practice partner settings you chose, your profile, messages exchanged during practice), and Read Their Mind (the situation text you selected or entered, and your gender, age, and MBTI). The content of these three features is not stored on the operator's side, whether or not you consented to Save Coaching Content (answers you report yourself are stored under "Reporting AI Answers" below). Data sent is processed under Google's Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, or, when sent to Anthropic or OpenAI, under the Anthropic Commercial Terms of Service and Privacy Policy or the OpenAI Business Terms and Privacy Policy.

In apps connected to the coach server (apps downloaded from the stores, and the web), a personal API key registered in settings is not used, and requests are sent through the relay server to Google (backup: Anthropic, OpenAI) as described above. Only in builds without the coach server, if you register a personal API key, requests are sent directly from your device to the provider of that key (Google Gemini API or Anthropic API) without going through the relay server, and that provider's terms and privacy policy (such as the Anthropic Privacy Policy) apply.

Before sending anything to AI, the App tells you where it will be sent and asks for your consent (latest app and web — earlier app versions send it without asking separately, so please update to the latest version). If you do not consent, you cannot use the AI features (reply ideas, Crush Report, Practice Chat, Read Their Mind), and you can withdraw your consent at any time with the AI Analysis Consent switch under My → Privacy.

Reporting AI Answers

If an AI-generated answer is offensive or inappropriate, you can report it right away without leaving the App (the flag button on reply cards; in Practice Chat, the flag at the top of the screen (for the other person's most recent message), long-pressing the other person's speech bubble, or the flag next to the coach's feedback; Report at the bottom of the Crush Report and Read Their Mind screens; My → Report an AI Answer). A report is sent only when you tap "Report," regardless of your Save Coaching Content consent.

  • What is sent: The content of the reported AI answer (may include parts of the conversation such as the other person's name; for Read Their Mind, also the situation text you asked about), the reason you chose and any note you wrote, feature type (reply ideas, Crush Report, Practice Chat, Read Their Mind), the AI that generated the answer (e.g., Google Gemini via the relay server), app version and device type (iOS, Android, web)
  • Purpose: Reviewing inappropriate AI answers and improving filters
  • Retention: Stored in the operator's database (Supabase, Seoul region, Republic of Korea) and automatically destroyed daily once 1 year has passed from the report date. Answers reported during a Private Chat are stored the same way, and the App tells you this before you report.
  • What is not stored: The device ID and IP address are not stored with the report, so no one can tell who sent it. Only to prevent excessive reporting, a hashed value of the IP address is kept briefly in server memory and then deleted.
  • Deletion requests: Because the sender cannot be identified, if there is a report you want deleted, please tell the Chief Privacy Officer below the date and content of the report. Once confirmed, we will delete it without delay.

3. Payment Information

Payments for passes (24-Hour Pass, Weekly Plan, 1-Year Pass, Credit Packs — including the Lifetime Pass sold previously) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The App does not collect or receive payment method information such as card numbers or bank accounts. To check whether a pass applies, the App checks the purchase history (product ID, transaction ID, expiration date) that the store provides to your device only on the device, and the App does not send this purchase history to the app server. 24-Hour Passes and Credit Packs are credited to the device used for payment, and transaction IDs are kept on the device so the same payment is not credited twice. However, the type of premium plan in use (weekly, 1-year, lifetime) and payment screen views and payment attempts and results (product type, store error messages) are included in the Service Usage Records in Section 4 for analyzing payment flows and payment errors. Payment method information such as card numbers is not included.

Store payment notifications: Separately, the App Store and Google Play send notifications to the app server when a payment, renewal, refund, scheduled cancellation, expiration, or payment failure occurs. After confirming that a notification was sent by the store, the app server keeps the "Store payment notifications" items in the Section 1 table (product, amount, transaction ID, etc.) in the database for 1 year from receipt, and notifies the operator's Telegram of the product and amount (see the international transfer table in Section 2). These notifications contain no name, email, or payment method information such as card numbers, and the app server does not link them to member information or device IDs.

4. Service Usage Records and (Optional) Saving Coaching Content

Service Usage Records (collected automatically for users 14 or older)

The App (version 1.0.4 or later) and the web store the "Service Usage Records" in the Section 1 table, which are generated automatically while you use them, in the operator's database. These records are processed to provide the service (making features work and verifying sessions), improve the service, analyze payment flows and payment errors, and prevent abuse. They are not collected on the basis of separate consent; they are processed with notice given through this Policy. The App's first screen and "My → Privacy → Privacy Settings" also explain this.

  • What is not collected: Your name/nickname, conversation text you type or paste, coach answers, and conversation screenshot images are not included in Service Usage Records. Phone numbers, addresses, advertising identifiers, and location information are not collected.
  • Users under 14: If you enter an age under 14 in your profile, records are not sent, and any records of that device remaining on the server are also deleted (Section 9).
  • How users are distinguished: By an ID that the App generates randomly on the device. If you sign up, usage records are linked to your member information. Advertising identifiers and phone numbers are not used, and usage records are not shared with other companies or used for advertising or tracking.
  • Requests to stop processing or delete: You can request suspension of processing or deletion via "My → Privacy → Privacy Settings → Request to Stop Processing / Delete" (this opens an email containing your device ID) or by emailing the Chief Privacy Officer in Section 15. Within 10 days of receiving the request, we delete that device's records, take measures so that records of a device that requested suspension of processing are not stored afterward, and then notify you of the result. Running "Delete All Data" at the bottom of My → Privacy also requests deletion of that device's records remaining on the server (latest app and web). If you cannot use the App, please make the request using the Data Deletion Request method.
  • Operator notifications: When you open the payment screen or proceed with a payment (entering the payment sheet, completion, cancellation, error), and when you finish the KKTI test, the items in the Section 1 table are sent to the operator's Telegram chat based on those usage records. These are internal notifications for promptly checking payment problems and KKTI usage, and are kept for 1 year from receipt (see the international transfer table in Section 2).
  • Retention period: Screen and button records and how you first arrived are automatically destroyed daily once 1 year has passed from collection; user information such as device ID, profile, and device type is automatically destroyed daily once 1 year has passed from the last day of use; and records are destroyed without delay upon a deletion request.
  • Earlier app versions: App versions 1.0.3 and earlier send usage records (including name/nickname and coaching content) only if you opted in to "Usage Record Collection" on the first screen or the My tab. Names/nicknames stored under that consent are deleted after you update to 1.0.4 or later. Records are not stored under old consents that were obtained with the box pre-checked; records that even earlier app or web versions already sent under such consents are destroyed when the retention period ends, and you can delete them sooner via "My → Privacy → Privacy Settings → Delete Previously Sent Usage Records" in the latest app or via a Data Deletion Request.

(Optional) Saving Coaching Content

Only if you opt in, the "Coaching content" in the Section 1 table is stored together with Service Usage Records to provide better coaching. You can use all features even if you do not consent.

  • Items: The other person's name/nickname, gender, age, MBTI, relationship stage, and tone in the coaching request; conversation text you type or paste (including the other person's messages); whether a screenshot was attached; the coach's answers (summary, interest level, recommended replies, next-step advice); response time and error information. The uploaded conversation screenshot images themselves are not stored.
  • Purpose: Improving coaching quality and analyzing errors (the operator may view it for improvement purposes)
  • Retention period: Automatically destroyed daily once 1 year has passed from collection, and destroyed without delay if you withdraw consent.
  • How consent is given: Consent is recognized only if you check "(Optional) Save Coaching Content" yourself in the first-screen consent prompt or tap "Agree to All and Start," or turn it on in "My → Privacy → Privacy Settings." All checkboxes in the consent prompt are shown unchecked, and you can start with optional items turned off. Coaching content of users who entered an age under 14 is not stored even if they consent. An opt-in to "Usage Record Collection" (including storage of coaching questions and answers) in app versions 1.0.3 and earlier carries over to this consent.
  • Withdrawing consent: If you turn off "Save Coaching Content" in "My → Privacy → Privacy Settings," storage stops immediately and the App requests deletion of that device's coaching content stored on the server. If you are offline, the App requests it again the next time it starts, and it also deletes records of coaching requests sent just before you turned it off that were stored late. Until deletion is complete, coaching content is not stored even if you consent again. Running "Delete All Data" also resets this consent to its initial state, and you will be asked again on the first screen (latest app and web).
  • Right to refuse: You may refuse consent, and refusing does not restrict your use of the App.

Processing outsourcing and international transfers: Database storage of Service Usage Records and coaching content is outsourced to Supabase Inc., and they are processed by US companies Vercel Inc. (relay server) and Supabase Inc. (storage, Seoul region). The recipients, contact details, timing and method of transfer, and retention periods are in the international transfer table in Section 2. If you sign up, how you first arrived is also delivered to the operator via Telegram as part of the operator notification (Section 5, "Operator notifications").

AI Server Operational Diagnostics (separate from Service Usage Records)

When processing AI requests, we record the feature type, model, response status and predefined error code, token count, number of attempts, finish reason, whether a screenshot was attached, and processing time in Vercel runtime logs to diagnose costs and outages. These structured diagnostic logs do not include conversation text, original photos, names, emails, member IDs, device IDs, or IP addresses. However, because they are runtime logs that Vercel manages per request, we do not claim that they are fully anonymous aggregates. Operational diagnostics for AI requests are recorded even if you do not consent to Save Coaching Content.

Under the current plan, the Runtime Logs retention is 1 hour, and external log storage integrations (Drains) are not used. This retention period applies regardless of account deletion or deletion of usage records and coaching content. We do not add member or device identifiers to these logs, including for the purpose of finding and deleting a particular member's records. This period does not mean that all of Vercel's internal security records and backups are destroyed at the same time.

13. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS). API keys you enter are kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore; browser storage on the web)
  • Database access control: reading and writing with the public key is blocked (row-level security), and access is possible only with the server's secret key
  • The admin screen opens only on registered browsers with a password, and password-guessing attempts are blocked
  • Conversation screenshots are not stored on the server, and users are distinguished by a random device ID created by the App instead of a phone number or advertising identifier
  • Member login is handled by Kakao, Apple, and Google, and the App does not receive passwords
  • To prevent excessive calls, a hashed value of the requesting device's IP address is kept briefly (about 15 minutes) in server memory and then deleted (it is not stored in usage records — for member login records, see Section 5)
  • Caps on daily storage and total database capacity, so the database cannot be filled up through the public app address
  • The people who handle personal information are kept to a minimum (the operator)
