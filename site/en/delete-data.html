Data Deletion Request

App: My Love Coach (Korean: 센딧) · Developer: CRAVING

Menu locations: Starting with app version 1.3.0, the My screen is organized by topic — Privacy Settings, AI Analysis Consent, and Delete All Data are under My → Privacy, and Delete Account is under My → Passes & Account. In earlier app versions, these items are directly on the My screen.

My Love Coach stores the originals of your conversation screenshots, chats, and profiles on your device. The operator's servers store the information described in the Privacy Policy, such as Service Usage Records (age 14 or older), coaching content you opted in to save, member information if you signed up, and AI answer reports you sent yourself. You can request deletion as described below.

1. Delete data stored on your device (immediately)

To reuse results for the same request, the App keeps copies of coaching results and Read Their Mind results on the device, separately from the recent history list. Tapping Delete All Data at the bottom of My → Privacy immediately deletes your chats, screenshots, profiles, practice history, Read Their Mind history, and the reuse copies, and also requests deletion of that device's usage records and coaching content remaining on the server (latest app and web; for earlier app versions, please request by email as described in Section 2 below). The number of free coaching sessions used, passes and transaction IDs used to prevent duplicate crediting, the device ID, how you first arrived, and your member login status remain on the device. You can delete your account separately in Section 3 below. AI Analysis Consent and Save Coaching Content consent are reset to their initial state and you will be asked again. Deleting the App deletes all of this as well.

2. Delete usage records and coaching content stored on the server

  1. Coaching content (questions and answers you opted in to save): If you turn off "Save Coaching Content" in My → Privacy → Privacy Settings, storage stops and the App requests deletion of that device's coaching content stored on the server. If the App cannot connect, it requests again the next time it starts (latest app and web).
  2. Service Usage Records (screen and button records, etc., collected automatically for users 14 or older): You can request deletion of that device's records remaining on the server with "Delete All Data" in Section 1 above. If you want processing stopped (no further records stored) or records deleted, please make the request via My → Privacy → Privacy Settings → Request to Stop Processing / Delete (this opens an email containing your device ID) or by the email below.
  3. Devices that sent records under a consent that was pre-checked on the first screen of an earlier version can delete them with My → Privacy → Privacy Settings → Delete Previously Sent Usage Records in the latest app.
  4. If you use an earlier version of the App or want to make a separate request, please send a "Data deletion request" (or "Request to stop processing") to the address below. If you can see My → My Device ID in the App, tap it to copy the ID and include it; if not, please include the approximate dates you used the App and the name (nickname) you entered in the App.
  5. After confirming, we will delete all records of that device within 7 days (and, if you requested suspension of processing, take measures so that no further records are stored) and reply to you.

3. Delete your account (if you signed up on the web or another version that supports sign-up)

If you use an existing store app version without a sign-up feature, please use the usage record deletion procedure above. Members who signed up with Kakao, Apple, or Google can delete their account as follows.

  1. In web and app versions that support sign-up, complete the confirmation steps in Delete Account at the bottom of My → Passes & Account. Your member information, login account, and the usage records of devices linked to your member account will be deleted.
  2. If you cannot use the App, send an "Account deletion request" to the email above, together with your sign-up method (Kakao, Apple, or Google) and the nickname or email you used when signing up. After verifying your identity, we will delete your account within 7 days and reply to you.

Deleting your account deletes your member information and login account in this App; it does not delete your Kakao, Apple, or Google account itself. Disconnecting from the login provider is also separate. For Google, turn off Sign in with Google for this app under connected apps in your Google Account; for Kakao, disconnect it in your Kakao Account's connected services management. Do not assume this connection is removed automatically when you delete your account in the App. Conversely, removing only the provider connection leaves the information already stored in the App, so please also complete the account deletion procedure above.

For Apple-linked accounts, if automatic disconnection cannot be completed, we keep your member information and let you try again. Alternatively, after reviewing Apple's manual disconnection instructions and acknowledging that automatic disconnection was not completed, you can request deletion of your App membership with a separate final confirmation. Completion of member deletion through the manual path does not mean that Apple's automatic disconnection succeeded.

Data deleted and retention periods

Deleting your account does not cancel auto-renewing subscriptions. If you are subscribed, please cancel separately in App Store subscription management or Google Play subscription management. You can also open the management screen from "My → Manage / Cancel Subscription" in the App.
