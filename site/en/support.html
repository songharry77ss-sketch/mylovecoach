Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?
Upload a screenshot of your conversation, and we'll read their reaction and suggest replies worth sending, along with an interest level. No screenshot? You can describe the situation in text instead.
Do I need to sign up?
Signing up is optional. You can try it first without an account, and on web and app versions that support sign-up, you can sign up with Kakao, Google, or (on supported iOS versions) Apple to get extra free coaching.
Where are my uploaded screenshots stored?
Only on your phone. They are sent briefly for AI analysis when we create replies, but we don't store screenshot images on our side. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
I want to delete my records.
In the latest web and app versions, "Delete All Data" at the bottom of the My screen deletes your chats, screenshots, profiles, practice history, and Read Their Mind results, and also requests deletion of the usage records and coaching content this device sent to our server. You can turn off Save Coaching Content (optional) in "My → Privacy Settings," and turning it off also deletes the stored coaching content. Copies of coaching and Read Their Mind results kept on the device for reuse with the same request are deleted as well. Your passes, usage counts, device ID, and member login status remain. Member information can be deleted separately with "Delete Account" at the bottom of the My screen. For the full scope and deletion requests for earlier versions, see Data Deletion.
Can I send the AI reply as is?
Replies are suggestions for reference. We recommend reading them once to make sure they fit your style and situation before sending.
Is it paid?
Web and app versions that support sign-up offer 1 trial session without an account, a 3-session sign-up bonus, and 1 session per day for members. Earlier app versions without sign-up offer 3 trial sessions and 1 per day. You can sign up with Kakao, Google, or (on supported iOS versions) Apple. To use it without limits, you can get a 24-Hour Pass, the Weekly Plan, or a 1-Year Pass (1 year from the date of purchase, no automatic renewal) in the App. A Lifetime Pass you bought earlier keeps working. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; deleting your account alone does not cancel it.
