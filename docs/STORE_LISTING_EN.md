## Full description

My Love Coach is an AI dating coach developed with psychology researchers at Korea University.
Not sure what to text back? From that first message to your crush, to making up after a fight — upload a screenshot of your chat or just describe the situation, and your coach reads the vibe and gives you 3 replies you can send right now, plus how interested they seem.

📸 One screenshot is all it takes
Upload a screenshot from any messaging or dating app. Your coach reads the conversation and suggests 3 different replies — compare them and copy one with a tap. Each comes with an estimated success rate (AI estimate) and their likely reaction.

✍️ No screenshot? Just describe it
"We had a great first date — how do I ask for a second one?" "They left me on read for 3 hours, should I text first?" Describe what's going on and get reply ideas and advice instantly.

🗣️ Sounds like you
Replies keep your texting style — your nicknames, your level of formality, your emoji habits. Replies come in the same language you text in.

💬 Perfect for when…
• Your crush left you on read and you're debating a double text
• You want a second date without sounding desperate
• You can't tell if they're flirting or just being nice
• They sent a dry reply and you're stuck
• You had a fight and want to make up first

🎯 Pick your vibe
Natural · Flirty · Witty · Cool · Sincere. Don't love the replies? Tap "More replies" for new ones.

🧠 A coach that remembers
Your coach remembers what you've shared in each chat and builds on it next time. You can see everything it remembers and delete any line, anytime.

🌡️ Interest level from 0° to 100°
Every person starts at 0°, and the heat goes up or down with each conversation. See where things stand at a glance — and get a Crush Report (personality, signals, strategy, compatibility) from your history.

🔮 Read their mind
"What are they thinking when they don't reply?" Pick a situation and see what's likely going on in their head, with probabilities and a message you could send.

🎮 Practice before the real thing
Rehearse with an AI practice partner that texts like a real person, and get instant feedback on every message. (Turn it on from the My tab.)

🧪 Texting Type Test
A 1-minute fun quiz that reveals your texting personality — and your compatibility with your crush once you both take it. Not affiliated with the official MBTI® assessment.

🤫 Private chat
Ask about things you'd rather not keep — private chats are not saved on your device.

🔒 Your data, your call
Use it without an account, or sign in with Google or Apple. Your chats and screenshots stay on your device. You can manage consent and delete your account from the My tab.

🤖 About the AI
Replies, readings and analysis are AI-generated suggestions — double-check they fit your situation before sending. Before anything is sent to an AI provider, we tell you where it goes and ask for your consent. Report any inappropriate answer with the report button on the result screen. People shown in store images are AI-generated, fictional characters.

💎 Optional passes
Get free coaching to start, then choose what you need:
• 24-hour pass — unlimited coaching for 24 hours (no auto-renewal)
• Weekly plan — unlimited coaching, renews weekly, cancel anytime
• 1-year pass — unlimited coaching for one year (no auto-renewal)
The weekly plan renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your store account at confirmation of purchase. Manage or cancel in your account settings. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.
